# Moonchrome — Visual Direction and Anti-Overlap Brief

**Status:** `implemented / not-human-reviewed` · **Task:** MF-VF-204 · **Scope:** precision futurist skin direction; no production token, font, logo, asset, or consumer-brand approval.

## Direction

Present Moonchrome as a precision instrument with a quiet, layered focal surface. Build the shell from a crisp masthead and restrained side stack, using thin contour lines and carefully bounded metallic cues to separate material tiers. Keep the central reading plane matte and opaque; reflective gradients belong at peripheral rims, never under labels, placeholders, values, or focus indicators. Use geometric display hierarchy with a highly legible modern body face and tabular numerals where useful. Use only fonts with reviewable licenses and retain platform fallbacks.

Make controls feel machined through a fine outline and a clean inset field, not heavy industrial plates. Cards can carry a narrow spectral edge around a neutral content core; dialogs keep their text plane flat. Tables favor precise alignment and neutral row backing, while gauges and orbital metrics always have text summaries and an accessible data table. Notifications pair any spectral edge with written status. These are composition rules, not approved colors or token values.

## Distinction from neighboring skins

- **Moonglitch:** Moonchrome uses calm, precise contours, layered material planes, and orbital metrics. Moonglitch uses diagnostic overlays, scanline geometry, and terminal-like data treatment. Do not add simulated signal errors or scan distortion to Moonchrome.
- **Mooncrash:** Moonchrome uses thin reflective boundaries and quiet focal depth. Mooncrash uses serviceable equipment zones, mechanical plates, and inspection flow. Avoid bolts, hazard striping, and rack-like work areas here.
- **Generic glass:** Keep text surfaces opaque and reflections peripheral. Do not turn the skin into translucent glass panels or rely on shine to establish hierarchy.

## Shared behavior and fallback

Preserve shared component APIs, DOM and focus order, keyboard behavior, form semantics, and ARIA meanings. Focus indicators and status text must remain clear in grayscale and forced colors. On narrow screens, remove reflective highlights before compressing content; keep controls and primary actions in reading order without horizontal page overflow. Reduced motion uses static material cues. Decoration-off mode retains the complete interface and every status meaning.

Compare identical fixtures in light and dark modes, grayscale, decoration-off, forced colors, and responsive viewports before claiming visual distinction. Contrast, the moonglitch comparison, licensed fonts/assets, human review, and acceptance remain pending.

# Moonscrawl — Visual Direction and Anti-Overlap Brief

**Status:** `implemented / not-human-reviewed` · **Task:** MF-VF-180 · **Scope:** reusable skin direction; no production tokens, font assets, logos, or consumer-brand approval.

## Direction

Treat Moonscrawl as a sketchbook workspace: a clear canvas organized by exploratory clusters, with margin notes and notebook-style navigation. Use draft-paper surfaces and restrained ruled construction to give the page a working-notebook feel. Keep hand-drawn arrows, circles, and callouts around precise functional controls; controls themselves retain stable geometry, readable labels, visible focus, and predictable hit areas. Hand-lettered styling belongs on short labels only, while body copy and data remain legible. Annotations may be optional, but static composition must communicate the complete hierarchy.

## Distinction from neighboring skins

Moonscrawl's exploratory clusters and ruled notebook scaffolding differ from Moonink's spacious composition and occasional expressive brush marks. It also differs from Moonzine's deliberate editorial paper collage: Moonscrawl organizes working notes around a canvas rather than arranging finished editorial spreads. Carry the distinction through composition, navigation, and annotation placement instead of relying on color or texture alone.

## Shared behavior and fallback

Preserve shared component APIs, DOM order, keyboard and form behavior, and ARIA meanings. Keep annotations out of interactive text, focus indicators, and status meaning. Decoration-off and forced-colors modes remove decorative marks without losing labels or structure; reduced motion uses the complete static layout. At narrow widths, move margin notes into a readable sequence without changing semantic order or obscuring controls. Human visual review, asset rights, and acceptance remain pending.

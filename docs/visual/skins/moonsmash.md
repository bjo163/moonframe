# Moonsmash — Visual Direction and Anti-Overlap Brief

**Status:** `implemented / not-human-reviewed` · **Task:** MF-VF-351 · **Scope:** campaign-poster art direction only; no production token, logo, asset, or consumer-brand approval.

## Direction

Moonsmash is a portrait campaign-poster composition built around one dominant focal point. Use a tall, aspect-preserving artboard with full-bleed, optional scene artwork; organize supporting title, event details, tags, and call to action as a deliberate top-to-center-to-bottom reading path. Angular overprint, radial impact marks, rough-edged paper shapes, and strong scale shifts may add energy, while the focal subject and live copy remain distinct layers. Keep essential wording as selectable DOM text wherever the poster is presented as a web page.

This is a composition recipe, not a default application shell. Do not apply its burst-centered hierarchy or full-bleed scene treatment to dashboards, forms, navigation, or general-purpose controls. Keep editing and preview controls outside the poster canvas, preserve a useful crop at narrow widths, and offer a no-art fallback. Motion is optional, honors reduced-motion preferences, and never carries essential meaning.

## Distinction from neighboring skins

- **Moonpunk:** Moonsmash centers a single poster focal point with radial energy; Moonpunk uses a repeatable framed utility shell, side rail, and block-built zones. Do not turn Moonsmash into a dashboard or make Moonpunk a poster burst.
- **Moonzine:** Moonsmash uses a concentrated stage-like focal hierarchy; Moonzine distributes attention across editorial stories and paper-cut collage. Keep poster metadata legible and subordinate to the focal image.
- **Moonriot:** Moonsmash radiates from a central focal point; Moonriot moves across an open page with directional editorial cuts. Do not use a poster composition as a general-purpose page layout.

## Content, accessibility, and review constraints

All performer imagery, names, lyrics, logos, and campaign copy are consumer-supplied content whose rights must be established by that consumer; this brief grants no reuse rights. Framework examples use generic placeholder copy and anonymous or abstract artwork only. A flattened poster export needs an equivalent text transcript or descriptive alternative. Keep reading order meaningful without artwork, maintain visible focus for any adjacent controls, and do not encode status or instructions by color alone.

Use the canonical skin grammar and specifications for implementation; this brief does not add a skin ID, approve token values or fonts, or change component behavior. Before claiming visual distinction, compare the same content in light and dark modes, grayscale, decoration-off, and responsive portrait previews. Visual parity and rights remain unreviewed until evidence is inspected by the appropriate human reviewer.

## Brand strategy alternatives — architecture judgment (not normative external fact)

| Model | Advantage | Risk | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 15 autonomous brands/logos | Personalization freedom | Fragmented brand, trademark/review cost × 15, unnecessary duplication | **Reject as default** |
| One generic mark recolored 15 times | Simple production | Fails independent grammar and 105 pairwise uniqueness objective | **Reject as sole identity** |
| **One Moonframe umbrella design-system + 15 visual editions** | Recognizable family, reusable package, distinct visuals, easy licensing and app co-branding | Requires per-skin proof and a clear brand lockup rule | **Maintainer decision recorded 2026-10-26 (structure only); no mark or rights clearance** |

### Current decisions and open gates

**Research-informed proposed:** master brand logo family shared; per-skin *signature motif and visual system* distinct; optional edition badge only when needed; native semantic icons remain the same regardless of skin; no artist/tool/vendor guarantees originality. **Open owner decisions:** brand/legal/trademark clearance, exact master mark, whether public repo/asset packs use separate licenses, font family/rights, favicon/platform matrix, sonic signature, paid providers and external brand co-branding. Tools are **candidates only**; exact SVGO optimizer command/config follows future vetted proof.
